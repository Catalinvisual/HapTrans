toll Why Highway Fuel Costs More

Motorway and highway service stations across Europe consistently charge a premium compared to city or town fuel stations. This "highway premium" is well-documented and affects millions of drivers daily.

landscape
Land & Concession Costs

Highway stations pay premium rates for strategic motorway locations and government concession fees.

schedule
24/7 Operations

Mandatory round-the-clock service requires additional staffing, security, and operational costs.

groups
Captive Market

Limited competition and traveler convenience allows for higher pricing—drivers often won't detour to find cheaper fuel.

map Regional Variations

🇮🇹
Italy (Autogrill)

Italian highway "Autogrill" stations charge €0.15–0.25 more per liter than local stations. A detour of just 5km off the autostrada can save significant money on a full tank.

🇬🇷
Greece

Price differences of up to 25% have been observed between highway stations and those just 5km into town—among the highest in Europe.

lightbulb
Money-Saving Tip: Plan your refueling stops before hitting the highway. Apps like Google Maps show fuel prices along your route. Filling up at supermarket stations or town centers before entering motorways can save €5-15 per tank.

What is Euro 95?

Euro 95 (RON 95) is the standard unleaded gasoline across Europe.

In US terms ≈ mid-grade / low-premium (90–91 AKI). Europe uses RON octane; US uses AKI, which is 4–6 points lower for the same fuel.

Fuel & Gas Prices in Europe – Current Data Summary

Last updated: June 15, 2026 · Source: European Commission Oil Bulletin · 27 EU member states

Current Fuel & Gas Prices in Europe (2026)

As of June 15, 2026, the average price of Euro 95 (Petrol) across all 27 EU member states is €1.771 per liter and the average price of diesel is €1.781 per liter. The price spread between the cheapest and most expensive EU countries is €1.052 per liter for petrol. Searching for gas prices in Europe? In US terms, gas (gasoline) is the same as Euro 95 petrol — so these are the current gas prices across Europe, updated weekly.

Cheapest Fuel Prices in Europe (2026)

The EU country with the cheapest Euro 95 (Petrol) as of June 15, 2026 is Malta at €1.340 per liter. The country with the cheapest diesel is Malta at €1.210 per liter. Lower fuel prices in Eastern Europe are primarily due to lower excise duty rates.

Most Expensive Fuel Prices in Europe (2026)

The EU country with the most expensive Euro 95 (Petrol) is Denmark at €2.392 per liter. The most expensive diesel is in Finland at €2.239 per liter. Higher prices in these countries reflect higher excise duties and VAT rates.

About This Data

fuel-prices.eu tracks Euro 95 (Petrol) and diesel prices across all 27 EU member states with weekly data going back to January 2005 — over 20 years of historical fuel price data. Prices are sourced from the European Commission's Weekly Oil Bulletin, which collects national average consumer prices (including all taxes) from official authorities in each member state. Data is available for download in CSV, JSON, and PDF formats.
